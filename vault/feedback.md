# Feedback rules

Gemini reads these on every run; they override its defaults. Each rule comes from a
correction. Keep them short and concrete, and delete rules that stop being true.

<!-- Example:
- Cloud cost-optimization tips go under `system-design/scalability`, not a new `devops` topic. (2026-09-28)
-->

- Game development content goes under `game-dev/*` (and its tools under `dev-tools/game-dev`), even when an AI assistant is how the work gets done. `ai-ml/*` is for building AI itself. (2026-09-30)
